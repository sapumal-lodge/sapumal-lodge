🥣 Breakfast at Sapumal Lodge

Start your day with a wholesome, homemade breakfast lovingly prepared using fresh, local ingredients. Whether you’re heading out for a day of exploring ancient Anuradhapura or just relaxing at the lodge, our breakfast is sure to energize you.

🍽️ What We Offer

  • 🥪 Freshly toasted bread and spreads
  • 🍳 Boiled eggs and omelettes
  • 🥞 Pancakes and Sri Lankan-style roti
  • 🧀 Butter, jam, and kiri pani (curd and treacle)
  • 🫕 Dhal curry, coconut sambol, and local condiments
  • 🍉 A colourful platter of seasonal tropical fruits
  • 🧃 Freshly made fruit juice (watermelon, papaya, or orange)
  • ☕ Ceylon tea or Nescafé

Vegetarian and child-friendly options are always available.
