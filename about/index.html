🏡 About Sapumal Lodge

We are the Dissanayake family, proud owners of Sapumal Lodge — a small, family-run hotel built with care, run with love, and maintained with pride.

Sapumal Lodge is not just a business, it’s the house we grew up in. Every wall, every room holds memories, and we’ve transformed this beloved family home into a peaceful haven for guests from all over the world.

We are passionate about providing a friendly, family-oriented service. Whether you’re visiting Anuradhapura for its sacred history or a quiet getaway, we want you to feel truly at home.

👥 Meet Our Team

👤 Lakshan – Our dedicated manager, known for his warmth, calm presence, and attention to detail. He is also the main person behind our delicious breakfast.

👤 Malinda – Always ready to help on the ground with a smile. Known for his hospitality and helpful nature, he makes sure the needs of our guests are always met.

👤 Asiri – Oversees the business, supports staff wellbeing, and ensures we meet all the legal and safety requirements needed to operate smoothly.

We care deeply about our guests and treat everyone with kindness and respect. Whether you’re a solo traveller, family, or pilgrim, we are here to make your stay comfortable and memorable.

🙏 Thank you for choosing Sapumal Lodge. We look forward to welcoming you!
