### 🐆 Wilpattu Private Safari

**Wilpattu National Park** is Sri Lanka’s largest wildlife reserve, renowned for its natural lakes (*villus*) and incredible biodiversity — including **leopards**, **elephants**, and **sloth bears**.

Just a short drive from Anuradhapura, Wilpattu offers peaceful, scenic safaris through forests and open plains, making it the perfect destination for **nature lovers** and **wildlife photographers**.
