Welcome to Sapumal Lodge!

🙏 Welcome to Sapumal Lodge

A family-run boutique hotel offering affordable luxury in the heart of Anuradhapura, just minutes from the sacred city.

We provide warm, safe, and welcoming accommodation in a lovingly maintained home with modern comfort, friendly service, and a peaceful atmosphere ideal for families, solo travellers, and pilgrims alike.

  • 🚿 En-suite bathroom with hot water
  • 📺 Digital TV
  • 🦟 Mosquito net (on request)
  • ☕ Free tea & coffee
  • 💧 Filtered drinking water in glass bottles

  • ♻️ Use of glass water bottles (plastic by request)

About Us

We are a small, family-run hotel built with care, run with love, and maintained with pride.

Sapumal Lodge is not just a business — it’s the house we grew up in. Every wall, every room holds memories, and we’ve transformed this beloved family home into a peaceful haven for guests from all over the world.

We are passionate about providing a friendly, family-oriented service. Whether you’re visiting Anuradhapura for its sacred history or a quiet getaway, we want you to feel truly at home.
