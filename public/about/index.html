About Us

Sapumal Lodge

May 14, 2025

We are a small, family-run hotel built with care, run with love, and maintained with pride.

Sapumal Lodge is not just a business — it’s the house we grew up in. Every wall, every room holds memories, and we’ve transformed this beloved family home into a peaceful haven for guests from all over the world.

We are passionate about providing a friendly, family-oriented service. Whether you’re visiting Anuradhapura for its sacred history or a quiet getaway, we want you to feel truly at home.

Meet Our Team

👤 Lakshan – Our dedicated manager, known for his warmth, calm presence, and attention to detail.

👤 Malinda – Always ready to help on the ground with a smile, and often behind our delicious home-style breakfasts.

We care deeply about our guests and treat everyone equally, with kindness and respect. Whether you’re a solo traveller, family, or pilgrim, we are here to make your stay comfortable and memorable.

Thank you for choosing Sapumal Lodge. We look forward to welcoming you!
