Welcome to Sapumal Lodge!

🙏 Welcome to Sapumal Lodge

A family-run boutique hotel offering affordable luxury in the heart of Anuradhapura, just minutes from the sacred city.

About Us

🏡 About Sapumal Lodge

We are the Dissanayake family, proud owners of Sapumal Lodge — a small, family-run hotel built with care, run with love, and maintained with pride.

Sapumal Lodge is not just a business, it’s the house we grew up in. Every wall, every room holds memories, and we’ve transformed this beloved family home into a peaceful haven for guests from all over the world.

We are passionate about providing a friendly, family-oriented service. Whether you’re visiting Anuradhapura for its sacred history or a quiet getaway, we want you to feel truly at home.

Breakfast

🥣 Breakfast at Sapumal Lodge

Start your day with a wholesome, homemade breakfast lovingly prepared using fresh, local ingredients. Whether you’re heading out for a day of exploring ancient Anuradhapura or just relaxing at the lodge, our breakfast is sure to energize you.

🍽️ What We Offer

  • 🥪 Freshly toasted bread and spreads
  • 🍳 Boiled eggs and omelettes
  • 🥞 Pancakes and Sri Lankan-style roti
  • 🧀 Butter, jam, and kiri pani (curd and treacle)
  • 🫕 Dhal curry, coconut sambol, and local condiments
  • 🍉 A colourful platter of seasonal tropical fruits
  • 🧃 Freshly made fruit juice (watermelon, papaya, or orange)
  • ☕ Ceylon tea or Nescafé

Vegetarian and child-friendly options are always available.
