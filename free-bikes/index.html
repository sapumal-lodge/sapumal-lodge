🚲 Explore Anuradhapura on Two Wheels — For Free!

As part of our commitment to sustainability and guest satisfaction, Sapumal Lodge offers free bicycle rentals to all our guests! This is one of our most-loved Go Green initiatives. It’s a perfect way to explore the sacred city at your own pace.

Whether you’re heading to the ancient ruins, nearby lakes, or just out for a relaxing ride, our well-maintained bikes provide a peaceful and healthy way to experience Anuradhapura.

🌿 Why Guests Love Our Free Bikes

  • 💸 Completely Free – No rental fee for Sapumal Lodge guests
  • 🧰 Well-Maintained – Regularly serviced for comfort and safety
  • 🗺️ Ideal for Sightseeing – Perfect for visiting temples, parks, and historical sites
  • 🍃 Eco-Friendly – Emissions-free, quiet, and gentle on the environment
  • 💖 Loved by Guests – One of our most popular guest perks!
